SESSION LESSONS
---------------
- Real footage broke every synthetic-tested assumption within one clip: ID
  switches, pose jitter, duplicate tracks. First real-data run > any amount
  of synthetic testing for state machines.
- Normalized hip velocity on a lying body jitters to ±5 BH/s — single-frame
  velocity needs corroborating evidence (upright-recency, sustained pairs).
- Dataset hosts die (UR Fall's old domain: DNS-dead). Record mirrors +
  licenses + SHA256s at download time; re-verify URLs before every plan.
- Auto-annotation under detector nondeterminism is fragile (±0.1 normalized
  between passes); human zone marking is the production-grade answer.
- Benchmark denominators decide everything: 5 min of ADL footage cannot
  measure a per-hour FP criterion — match the metric to the corpus or say so.
- cv2 + PyAV co-load registers duplicate avdevice dylibs → intermittent
  segfault with no traceback. Defer the import; -u + PYTHONFAULTHANDLER=1
  for background runs.
